# For WINTER — 写给你的25岁

本轮新增：生成的雪峰溪谷信件背景、贴合两位女孩轮廓的紫色透明抠图、拼图烟花结束后的实际页面拍照与手机锁屏，以及 [`/alpine/`](dist/alpine/index.html) 三维雪峰小镇。手机按下快门会重新拍取已拼好的 DOM 页面，锁屏为 **12月15日 00:00**，显示 **Gwen · 现在：生日快乐，以后的每一个生日都想陪你过。** 小镇包含溪流跌水、木屋工坊、钟楼、缆车、牧场牛羊、像素居民、天气与三种时光，支持拖动/双指观察与点地面散步。

原生日内容、音乐、22 张便签、国家回忆、相册和独立深圳样章均保留。[实现与验证边界](docs/ALPINE-KEEPSAKE.md) · [生成素材记录](docs/ALPINE-ART-PROVENANCE.md)。当前环境无法使用浏览器预览，实际 WebGL 画面、拍照成片及 iPad 实机手感尚未验证；模拟、真实几何射线与资源检查不能替代这些实测。

A birthday gift made for WINTER by Gwen. The light translucent navigation switches among About me, Home, and Destination. Its single shared material uses route color variables; the night scene uses a quiet blue tint without glossy gradients or prism highlights. Home keeps the forest cover, with “For you,” above the moss-green WINTER title and Gwen's small dedication. The envelope, greeting and country letters also identify Gwen as the gift's sender.

About me is “Dear me”: her own words to her 25-year-old self. It contains 22 lightly creased sticky notes arranged as the digits 25: 11 notes for each digit. Each has a unique paper texture, fold paths, curl position, placement and shadow; the two digits do not repeat the same rotations. The double diagram-like frame has been removed. Slightly varied scale and stronger individual offsets let the papers feel placed by hand, with a quieter, desaturated botanical background and peripheral Alpine prints on wide screens. The existing portrait background is retained for phones. The requested self-addressed dedication remains underneath: “周琬婷，辛苦了。谢谢你过去的24年的坚韧。”

All notes start blank. Clicking a note opens an editor. Writing is stored only in the current browser under `winter-about-notes-v1`; it is not synchronized across devices or published into the Site source. Clearing a note's text and saving leaves it blank again. This update preserves the existing storage key and note IDs.

Destination uses its own dreamlike, AI-generated mountain / forest / meadow landscape. On entry, a native dialog says “生日快乐，我最珍惜的人” with one “谢谢你” button, which dismisses the dialog and fades in the birthday envelope. Escape also dismisses the greeting accessibly. A dusty sage/hemp cord wraps the envelope. The knot is the actual button: clicking it unthreads the bow, opens the flap and expands a paper sheet into the next view. The three phases are cancelled if the visitor leaves; system reduced motion skips the visual delay.

The sequence is: greeting → tied envelope → birthday word puzzle → three-tier cake, candles and fireworks → country memories. The puzzle view at `#unlock` frames a generated snowy / soft-aurora landscape between two torn-paper edges. It shows “happy” plus a blank input, with “WINTERRRR” underneath. Submitting BIRTHDAY in any letter case opens the candle ceremony. Wrong answers receive a gentle inline hint. The solved state lasts for the browser tab via `winter-birthday-word-solved`; direct visits to later steps respect the puzzle and ceremony gates.

The puzzle includes original loose-line artwork: a birthday cake with dimensional “25” candles and two women holding hands, both wearing the original right-hand figure's hairstyle. Flame bases and wicks meet directly above the digits. The cake's olive/ivory invitation now says “点我，收下祝福” with a hand icon, a smaller “蛋糕里藏了一句话” line and three gentle attention pulses when motion is enabled. It opens a native dialog with two speech bubbles: the exact requested birthday wish and a happy kaomoji, without bypassing the word puzzle.

The `#cake` scene now uses a complete photographic composition edited from the original ornate ivory three-tier cake and alpine lake references. Silver filigree, pearls, white flowers and sage foliage remain; the cake rests on a real-looking weathered stone plinth, with cool night ambient light, restrained cream highlights, contact shadows and a slight reflection. It is not rendered as a cutout over a separate landscape. Matching empty plates let the cake dissolve into the same camera view before the gratitude message and fireworks. Landscape and portrait photographs have separately aligned SVG candle coordinates. A single geometry function maps the image, wicks, tool landing and hit region together; very wide windows use the complete composition within a quiet navy field. The desktop title sits beside the cake; the phone title sits above it.

The larger silver flip-top lighter is a generated photographic cutout with cool blue metal reflections, a visibly hinged open lid, flint wheel and exposed wick. Its SVG flame is attached to the photographed wick, with a small contact shadow at the stone landing. Pointer capture supports mouse, pen and touch: pick it up, drag its flame toward the candles and dwell briefly, or release near them to ignite. Moving away returns it to the stone. A touch visitor can also tap the lighter, then tap the candle region. Enter/Space and non-PointerEvent browsers retain the original button ignition path. Escape, pointer cancellation, navigation, hiding the document and resizing clear capture, proximity timers and queued drag frames. No microphone is needed.

## Motion treatment

This update follows the user-requested [Ai-Design-Skill animation-systems](https://github.com/MuhammadimYusoff/Ai-Design-Skill/blob/main/agent-skills/web-design/animation-systems/SKILL.md), with its [ambient-section-particles](https://github.com/MuhammadimYusoff/Ai-Design-Skill/blob/main/agent-skills/web-design/ambient-section-particles/SKILL.md) guidance for the small light layer. The skill files were read from their public source; no global plugin, framework or animation dependency was installed.

The original Alpine cover, country stories and note IDs/storage are retained. `motion.css` and `motion.js` provide the shared enhancement/lifecycle layer; `celebration.css` adds the later user-requested visible clouds, untying/unfolding, puppy gallop, candle flames and vintage composition. The small header icon pauses the ambient motion and remembers that preference independently from the note contents; fireworks use the still fallback when it is off.

Motion tokens: 160 ms hover/press feedback, 240 ms small popovers, 480–600 ms entrances, and 950 ms for the main title. The easing is `cubic-bezier(.22,.72,.22,1)`. The home title appears before its supporting copy; paper notes enter with 26 ms spacing on desktop and 17 ms on mobile. Translations are small (8–12 px for entrances; a maximum of 4 px horizontally and 3 px vertically for mouse-driven scenery). The paper's authored rotation uses `transform`, while entrance movement uses the independent `translate` property.

The envelope atmosphere contains at most eight small lights at the outer edges. Home has two independently translating, true-alpha cloud banks; the image itself remains the original Alpine photograph. The night scene adds a near-invisible 72-second cloud drift and slow lake-light variation; the underlying photograph stays fixed so the candle alignment cannot drift. Puppy limbs, tails and travel use CSS motion only. Visible light density is based on container area; pointer movement shares a single queued frame and uses cached bounds. Lighter movement likewise queues at most one frame and does no per-move layout reads. The ambient layer has no permanent JavaScript loop. The finite fireworks sequence runs only during its active scene, caps its particles and pixel ratio, pauses its frames while the document is hidden and cancels all work when leaving. Decorative motion pauses while a dialog is open, the document is hidden or the Site is out of view. Observer subscriptions, listeners and pending frames are cleaned on page exit; back/forward cache restores resume without adding duplicates.

System `prefers-reduced-motion` disables the added effects, and touch/coarse-pointer input does not drive parallax. Direct manipulation of the lighter remains functional. Motion preferences use `winter-ambient-motion-paused`, preserving `winter-about-notes-v1` untouched. Validation covers the original interactions, mouse/touch drag and dwell/release ignition, touch tap-then-candle, keyboard fallback, pointer cancellation, 11 desktop/iPad/phone geometry cases, guarded ceremony routing, exact candle timing, text-to-particle sampling, canvas/reduced-motion fallbacks, all six country notes and animation cleanup. These are DOM/canvas and geometry checks; browser rendering and live performance profiling were unavailable in this environment. The linked conversation's screenshots could not be retrieved because no thread-reading tool was available; the detailed brief and actual project assets guided the work.

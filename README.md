# For WINTER — 写给你的25岁

A birthday gift made for WINTER by Gwen. The glass navigation switches among About me, Home, and Destination. Home keeps the forest cover, with “For you,” above the moss-green WINTER title and Gwen's small dedication. The envelope, greeting and country letters also identify Gwen as the gift's sender.

About me is “Dear me”: her own words to her 25-year-old self. It contains 22 lightly creased sticky notes arranged as the digits 25: 11 notes for each digit. Each has a unique paper texture, fold paths, curl position, placement and shadow; the two digits do not repeat the same rotations. The notes now sit in a botanical keepsake album: a generated photographic background of handmade paper, eucalyptus, ferns, wildflowers, sage silk and antique gold details, fine double borders, a small botanical flourish and peripheral Alpine photo prints on wide screens. A dedicated portrait generation preserves the framing on phones. The requested self-addressed dedication remains underneath: “周琬婷，辛苦了。谢谢你过去的24年的坚韧。”

All notes start blank. Clicking a note opens an editor. Writing is stored only in the current browser under `winter-about-notes-v1`; it is not synchronized across devices or published into the Site source. Clearing a note's text and saving leaves it blank again. This update preserves the existing storage key and note IDs.

Destination uses its own dreamlike, AI-generated mountain / forest / meadow landscape. On entry, a native dialog says “生日快乐，我最珍惜的人” with one “谢谢你” button, which dismisses the dialog and fades in the birthday envelope. Escape also dismisses the greeting accessibly. A dusty sage/hemp cord wraps the envelope. The knot is the actual button: clicking it unthreads the bow, opens the flap and expands a paper sheet into the next view. The three phases are cancelled if the visitor leaves; system reduced motion skips the visual delay.

The sequence is: greeting → tied envelope → birthday word puzzle → three-tier cake, candles and fireworks → country memories. The puzzle view at `#unlock` frames a generated snowy / soft-aurora landscape between two torn-paper edges. It shows “happy” plus a blank input, with “WINTERRRR” underneath. Submitting BIRTHDAY in any letter case opens the candle ceremony. Wrong answers receive a gentle inline hint. The solved state lasts for the browser tab via `winter-birthday-word-solved`; direct visits to later steps respect the puzzle and ceremony gates.

The puzzle includes original loose-line artwork: a birthday cake with dimensional “25” candles and two women holding hands, both wearing the original right-hand figure's hairstyle. Flame bases and wicks meet directly above the digits. The cake's olive/ivory invitation now says “点我，收下祝福” with a hand icon, a smaller “蛋糕里藏了一句话” line and three gentle attention pulses when motion is enabled. It opens a native dialog with two speech bubbles: the exact requested birthday wish and a happy kaomoji, without bypassing the word puzzle.

The `#cake` scene uses a generated transparent image of an ornate ivory three-tier cake with silver filigree, pearls, white flowers and sage foliage. Five SVG candles sit on its upper surface. Its background is now a generated photorealistic alpine lake at blue hour: snow peaks, pine forest, water reflections and broad dark-blue sky. The same full-bleed landscape continues behind the gratitude message and fireworks, replacing the earlier flat mountain drawing. Clicking the silver lighter ignites the candles and removes the lighter. A native dialog says “尊贵的小寿星winter请吹一下蜡烛”; confirming “好的，我知道啦” extinguishes the flames exactly 1,500 ms later. Five English gratitude lines remain for 10 seconds, then their sampled letter shapes scatter into particles and a 13-second fireworks show. A visible button explicitly continues to the memories. Canvas failure and reduced motion keep the English message readable with a still festival and the same continue action. The ceremony completion flag is tab-local under `winter-birthday-celebrated`.

The country memories keep the envelope's cream taped papers over a new photorealistic natural panorama: a blue alpine lake, snow peaks, leafy branches, pine trees, sunny grass and cloud-filled sky. The centered heading remains “两个国家，都有和你一起的时光”. China opens by default, with Britain and China flags switching the notes. Each country has three notes: its original story followed by the two paragraphs supplied by the user. Clicking the paper or pressing Enter/Space advances it; the counter explains how to continue and the third note wraps to the first. Each country keeps its own position while the page is open. The flags sit in a translucent paper-toned holder; the letter itself stays opaque for readability. On desktop the flags and letter form a centered two-column composition; on mobile the flags sit above a full-width note. Two original SVG puppies run across a sage-green grass strip below the memories with independently animated legs and tails. All story copy and the visible country labels, including the user-supplied “UnitedKindom” spelling, are preserved verbatim. Returning from the letter to the envelope does not replay the greeting; entering Destination afresh from another tab does.

Buildless HTML/CSS/JavaScript; deployable assets live in `dist/`. Responsive layouts, accessible tab controls, keyboard navigation, native dialogs and reduced-motion preferences are supported. Navigation supports `#home`, `#about`, `#destination`, `#unlock`, `#cake` and `#letter`, including browser back/forward. “只看风景” remains available on Home. `cake-scene.html` is the ceremony's source fragment, embedded in `index.html`; its styles and lifecycle are isolated in `cake-scene.css` / `cake-scene.js`. `memory-notes.js` handles the two independent stacks. `nature-atlas.css` contains the latest photographic scenery, album details and interaction-hint treatments; responsive image sources serve locally optimized WebP variants.

## Motion treatment

This update follows the user-requested [Ai-Design-Skill animation-systems](https://github.com/MuhammadimYusoff/Ai-Design-Skill/blob/main/agent-skills/web-design/animation-systems/SKILL.md), with its [ambient-section-particles](https://github.com/MuhammadimYusoff/Ai-Design-Skill/blob/main/agent-skills/web-design/ambient-section-particles/SKILL.md) guidance for the small light layer. The skill files were read from their public source; no global plugin, framework or animation dependency was installed.

The original Alpine cover, country stories and note IDs/storage are retained. `motion.css` and `motion.js` provide the shared enhancement/lifecycle layer; `celebration.css` adds the later user-requested visible clouds, untying/unfolding, puppy gallop, candle flames and vintage composition. The small header icon pauses the ambient motion and remembers that preference independently from the note contents; fireworks use the still fallback when it is off.

Motion tokens: 160 ms hover/press feedback, 240 ms small popovers, 480–600 ms entrances, and 950 ms for the main title. The easing is `cubic-bezier(.22,.72,.22,1)`. The home title appears before its supporting copy; paper notes enter with 26 ms spacing on desktop and 17 ms on mobile. Translations are small (8–12 px for entrances; a maximum of 4 px horizontally and 3 px vertically for mouse-driven scenery). The paper's authored rotation uses `transform`, while entrance movement uses the independent `translate` property.

The envelope atmosphere contains at most eight small lights at the outer edges. Home has two independently translating, true-alpha cloud banks; the image itself remains the original Alpine photograph. Puppy limbs, tails and travel use CSS motion only. Visible light density is based on container area; pointer movement shares a single queued frame and uses cached bounds. The ambient layer has no permanent JavaScript loop. The finite fireworks sequence runs only during its active scene, caps its particles and pixel ratio, pauses its frames while the document is hidden and cancels all work when leaving. Decorative motion pauses while a dialog is open, the document is hidden or the Site is out of view. Observer subscriptions, listeners and pending frames are cleaned on page exit; back/forward cache restores resume without adding duplicates.

System `prefers-reduced-motion` disables the added effects, and touch/coarse-pointer input does not drive parallax. Motion preferences use `winter-ambient-motion-paused`, preserving `winter-about-notes-v1` untouched. Validation covers the original interactions, guarded ceremony routing, exact candle timing, text-to-particle sampling, canvas/reduced-motion fallbacks, all six country notes and animation cleanup. These are DOM/canvas logic checks; browser rendering and live performance profiling were unavailable in this environment.
